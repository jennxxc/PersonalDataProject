-- File details --

MTAG_results.txt.gz
Output from and MTAG analysis based on answers to the following questions:
1) “Do you often feel lonely?”
2) “Including yourself, how many people are living together in your household?"
3) "How often do you visit friends or family or have them visit you?"
4) “How often are you able to confide in someone close to you?”
Details of the coding can be found in the methods section of the paper.

Loneliness_EurRel_Imputed.txt.gz
Output from BOLT-LLM based on answers to the following question:
1) “Do you often feel lonely?”

Pub_EurRel_Imputed.txt.gz
Sport_EurRel_Imputed.txt.gz
Rel_EurRel_Imputed.txt.gz
Output from BOLT_LLM based on answer to the following question:
1) "Which of the following do you attend once a week or more often? (you can 
select more than one)" response options were: “sports club or gym”, “pub or 
social club”, “religious group”, “adult education class”, and “other group 
activity”.

-- Further details --

All files were based on a sample of white-Europeans from UK Biobank, this 
sample included related individuals. For more details about the data set
please refer to the manuscript - "Elucidating the genetic basis of social 
interaction and isolation" Nature Communications 2018.
