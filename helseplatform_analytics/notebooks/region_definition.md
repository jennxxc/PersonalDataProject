# Central Norway Region Definition


This project focuses on municipalities belonging to the Central Norway healthcare region (Helse Midt-Norge).


The region includes:

## Trøndelag County

Examples:

- Trondheim
- Stjørdal
- Levanger
- Namsos
- Røros


## Møre og Romsdal County

Examples:

- Ålesund
- Molde
- Kristiansund


The analysis unit is:

Municipality-year level


Example:

| Municipality | Year |
|---|---|
| Trondheim | 2025 |
| Molde | 2025 |
